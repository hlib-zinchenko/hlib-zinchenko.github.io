(===) Pancakes

Classic fluffy pancakes. Makes ~8–10, ready in 15 min. Breakfast ONLY!!!

\_/ Ingredients

Dry:

  • Flour: 1 cup (~162 g)
  • Baking powder: 1 tsp
  • Sugar: 1 tbsp
  • Salt: a pinch

Wet:

  • Milk: 1 cup (~300 ml)
  • Egg: 1
  • Oil or melted butter: 1 tbsp (+ a little for the pan)

[~] Method

  1. Dry. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
  2. Wet. Add the egg, milk and oil.
  3. Batter (1 min). Whisk until it's as thick as sour cream. Small lumps are fine.
  4. Pan. Heat over medium and grease lightly with oil.
  5. Pour. ~2–3 tbsp of batter per pancake.
  6. Fry (~3 min). ~1–2 min until bubbles appear, flip, then ~1 min more.

(!) Notes

  • Don't mix until completely smooth, or the pancakes will be less fluffy.

[=#=] Lasagna with bolognese and béchamel

A two-day job. Day 1: bolognese, and part of it goes on 3 servings of pasta. Day 2: béchamel and lasagna from the rest of the bolognese. Pan: 22×16 cm.

\_/ Ingredients

Bolognese:

  • Minced meat (beef, or beef and pork): 500 g
  • Onions: ~2
  • Carrot: 1
  • Celery stalks: same amount as the carrot (onion:carrot:celery = 2:1:1)
  • Garlic: to taste
  • Olive oil
  • Butter: a small knob
  • Tomato paste: 1–2 tbsp
  • Dry red wine: 100–150 ml
  • Canned peeled tomatoes: 1 can
  • Salt, pepper, Italian herbs (optional)

Béchamel:

  • Butter: 50 g
  • Wheat flour: 50 g (2 tbsp)
  • Milk: 500 ml
  • Nutmeg: a pinch
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Assembly:

  • Dry lasagna sheets: as many as fit the pan
  • Cheese (mozzarella/sulguni): 150 g for the whole lasagna

[~] Method

Day 1: bolognese (3+ hours in total)

  1. Vegetables (15–22 min). Heat a pan over medium, add olive oil and a small knob of butter. Add the onion and fry for 5–7 min until translucent. Add the carrot and celery and fry for another 10–15 min over medium heat, stirring. The vegetables should turn soft and slightly caramelized. Take them out and set aside.
  2. Meat (a few min). In the same pan, heat a little more oil. Add the mince and don't touch it for 2–3 min, so a crust forms. Then break up the lumps and keep frying. If the meat lets out a lot of liquid, don't stew it: boil the liquid off over high heat.
  3. Bringing it together. When the meat has browned, return the vegetables and stir. Add a little olive oil and fry the garlic. Add the tomato paste and fry for 1 min. Pour in the wine and reduce it almost completely.
  4. Simmer (2–3 hours). Add the tomatoes and simmer for 2–3 hours. At this stage add salt, pepper and, if you like, Italian herbs.
  5. Divide. Use part of the bolognese for 3 servings of pasta. Keep the rest for the lasagna.

Day 2: béchamel

  1. Béchamel (~4 min + cooling). Melt the butter, add the flour and toast for 1.5 min. Pour in all the milk at once and stir vigorously, so there are no lumps. Cook for 2 min until thickened. Don't overdo it, or it gets too thick. Add the nutmeg, salt and pepper. Let it cool with plastic wrap pressed right onto the surface.

Assembly and baking

  1. Assembly. Spread a little béchamel on the bottom of the pan. Then repeat 4 times:
    1. Dry lasagna sheets
    2. Bolognese
    3. Béchamel
    4. Cheese
  2. Baking (40 min). Bake at 190 °C for 40 min. Let it cool a little before serving, otherwise you won't get it onto a plate.

(oo) Fried pork with onions

Quick pork with a crust, then braised with onions. ~20–25 min in total.

\_/ Ingredients

  • Pork: 500 g
  • Onions: to taste (more is better, just don't overdo it)
  • Smoked paprika (optional)
  • Sugar: a little (optional)
  • Fat for frying: oil or lard
  • Salt
  • Butter (at the end)

Fat, salt and butter: by eye!

[~] Method

  1. Heat up (2 min). Heat the pan with the fat until it just starts to smoke.
  2. Fry the meat (5–6 min, high heat). Don't touch the meat for the first 3 min on one side. Flip and fry for another 2–3 min until crusted.
  3. Onions (5–7 min, medium heat). Salt the meat. Add the onions, sprinkle them with smoked paprika and a little sugar if you like, and fry uncovered, stirring constantly, until the onions collapse, release their juice and turn golden.
  4. Braise (7–10 min, low heat). Cover with a lid. The meat turns tender and the onions caramelize.
  5. Finish (1 min, low heat). Take off the lid, melt the butter, stir and turn off the heat.

(!) Notes

  • A cast-iron pan is better.
  • Salt only after the crust, at step 3.

\~~~/ Kharcho with chicken broth (3 L)

A thick, sour and spicy soup on ready-made broth from two chicken legs. About an hour in total.

\_/ Ingredients

Main:

  • Chicken broth: ~2 L (if less, top up with water)
  • Meat from two chicken legs, picked off the bone
  • Potatoes: 3 medium (~450 g), in 1.5 cm cubes
  • Round rice: 70 g

Base:

  • Onions: 2 large (~250 g)
  • Garlic: 4–5 cloves (~20 g)
  • Tomato paste: 30 g
  • Oil: 30 ml (~2 tbsp)

Sourness and spices:

  • Pickled plums, pitted: 100 g (or the substitute below)
  • Walnuts, crushed: 50 g
  • Kharcho spice mix: ~3 g (1 tsp), start with this
  • Svan salt: ~6 g (1 tsp), at the end, to taste
  • Black pepper: ~1 g

[~] Method

  1. Prep (10 min). Put the broth on medium heat. Pick the meat off the legs and return it to the broth. Rinse the rice until the water is almost clear. Cut up the potatoes and onions.
  2. Frying (10 min). Heat the oil and fry the onions until golden. Add the tomato paste, stir and fry for a few more minutes.
  3. Potatoes and rice (15 min). When the broth comes to a boil, add the potatoes and rice. Reduce the heat and cook for 15 min. Check the potatoes: if they're still hard, add 5 min. If the soup is too thick, top up with hot water.
  4. Onions and plums (10 min). Tip the fried onions and the mashed plums into the soup. Cook over low heat.
  5. Spices, walnuts, garlic (3 min). Add the walnuts, garlic, spice mix and pepper. Stir and cook for 3 more min.
  6. Taste and rest (10 min). Adjust with Svan salt, a little at a time. Not sour enough: more plums or lemon juice. Not hot enough: a little more spice. Turn off the heat and cover with a lid for 10 min.

<=> No plums?

Replace the 100 g of plums with:

  • 20 g more tomato paste (50 g in total), added at step 2
  • Lemon juice: 20–30 ml, added at the end of step 6, tasting every 10 ml
  • A pinch of sugar, if it turns out too sour
  • Salt: the plums were salty, so without them you need more. Add ~5 g more ordinary salt at step 6, tasting as you go.

(!) Notes

  • Svan salt and the plums are salty, so don't salt it at the start.
  • Add the spices and salt gradually: the mix is hot.
  • Kharcho tastes even better the next day.

\o~o/ Italian minestrone (3 L)

A proper thick, fragrant and rich vegetable soup.

\_/ Ingredients

Main:

  • Dry beans: 100–120 g (~250–300 g once boiled)
  • Small pasta: 60–80 g (ditalini, orzo or small elbows)
  • Potatoes: 2 medium (~200–250 g)
  • Zucchini: 1 small (~150–200 g)
  • Spinach or kale: 1 handful (~30–50 g)

Vegetable base (soffritto):

  • Onion: 1 large (~150 g)
  • Carrot: 1 medium (~120 g)
  • Celery stalks: 2 (~80–100 g)
  • Garlic: 2–3 cloves (~10–15 g)
  • Olive oil: 2–3 tbsp (~30–40 ml)

Sauce, liquid and spices:

  • Tomatoes in their own juice: 300–400 g
  • Tomato paste: 1 tbsp (~30 g)
  • Water or vegetable broth: ~1.5–1.8 L
  • Spices: 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp basil, salt (~8–10 g) and black pepper to taste

To serve:

  • Grated parmesan (~30–50 g)
  • Fresh olive oil
  • Crusty bread / ciabatta

=|-- Prep

  1. Soak the beans. Cover the dry beans with cold water for at least 6–8 hours.
  2. Boil the beans. Drain, rinse, cover with fresh water and boil until done (about 40–60 min).
  3. Chop. Cut the onion, carrot, celery, potatoes and zucchini into small, even cubes.

[~] Method

  1. Vegetable base (soffritto). Heat the olive oil in a heavy-bottomed 3 L pot. Add the onion, carrot and celery. Fry over medium heat for 8–10 min until soft.
  2. Aroma. Add the chopped garlic and 1 tbsp of tomato paste. Stir for 1–2 min, so the paste opens up its color and aroma.
  3. Vegetables in. Add the potatoes, zucchini and crushed tomatoes in their own juice. Pour in hot broth or water (leave a little room at the top of the pot for the pasta and beans). Salt, add the oregano and basil. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat and simmer covered for 15 min.
  4. Final stage. Add the cooked beans and the small pasta. Cook until the pasta is done (roughly 8–10 min, depending on the package). Add the chopped spinach 2 min before turning off the heat.

(!) Tips for the perfect flavor

  • For thickness: take 1–2 tbsp of the cooked beans, mash them with a fork and return them to the pot.
  • Texture: the soup should be thick. If the pasta soaks up too much liquid, just add a little boiling water.
  • Serving: serve hot, generously sprinkled with parmesan and with a drop of olive oil.
